# Scenes and canvas editing

Record and Livestream use a compact category chooser. A project can create or add
up to three scenes; older saved projects retain their existing scenes. The scene
summary and Create project remain visible while categories change.

Tutorial contains **Repertoire Mode** (acoustic piano, repertoire and annotation)
and **Theory Mode** (digital piano, harmony, notation and keyboard visualization).
Repertoire adds **Player Piano**, with a camera, right-side annotation, keyboard
visualization and overhead view. Its card specifies Yamaha Disklavier and Steinway
Spirio only. This is a compatibility note, not automatic device identification.

Previews share the session's keyboard and VexFlow notation renderers. Camera views
use standard person icons and their numbered names. Overhead previews show the
same keyboard at half opacity with hand icons. Interpreter previews show the
score above the camera and state that they use Score Sync; the recording itself
keeps the camera unobstructed for that shared post-recording workflow.

Canvas selection uses eight square resize handles with larger pointer targets.
Corner scaling preserves content proportions; side handles change the slot.
At minimum size, the opposite edge stays anchored. Snapping includes neighboring
element edges, centers and dimensions as well as output edges/centers. Hold Alt
to suppress snapping. Dashed purple guides show the matched axes.

Right-click an overlap to choose its layer, or Ctrl/Command-click to cycle through
layers at the pointer. Selecting a rear layer does not change its stacking order;
its handles draw above the other elements so they remain usable. The Layers list
also selects any layer. Focused resize handles support arrow keys (Shift: 10 px).
